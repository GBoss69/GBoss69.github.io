About the game

Professor Blot drained every color from the comic book. Play as Doodle, a little drop of cyan ink with a splatter cannon, across 25 levels in 5 worlds. Splat the Inkvaders, free the caged friends and paint every page back to life.

Controls

When you tap PLAY, the game asks which controls you want.

  • Joystick: the thumbstick on the left moves Doodle, into and out of the screen too. The buttons on the right are JUMP, FIRE, DASH and SPECIAL. Shots lock on to the nearest enemy you face.
  • Swipe: drag on the left half to move. Doodle fires automatically at the nearest enemy. On the right half, tap to jump (hold to jump higher), swipe sideways to dash, swipe down for Full Spectrum and swipe up to switch weapons.
  • Game controller: stick to move, A jump, X fire, B dash, Y special.

How to play

  • Every page starts gray. Splat enemies, grab the 3 pigment caches and free the caged friend to restore color.
  • At 25% the background colors in, at 50% color gates open, at 75% the music joins in, and at 100% you can visit the level's Secret Memory.
  • Lives are infinite and checkpoints save your spot. Earn 3 stars per level: finish it, reach 100% color, and lose 2 lives or fewer.
  • Collect coins to buy upgrades and outfits. New weapons unlock as you go.

Common questions

The game asks me to turn my phone. The title screen is upright; the levels play sideways. Turn your phone once you've picked your controls. If nothing happens, check that Rotation Lock is off in Control Center.

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your progress. There are no accounts and no cloud saves.

The game feels slow or my phone gets warm. Turn on Performance mode in Settings (pause menu or main menu). Closing other apps also helps.

No sound? Check the volume sliders in Settings and make sure your phone isn't on silent.

Does it need the internet? No. INKVADERS 3D plays fully offline. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.
