About the game

High above the clouds, floating garden islands hide hedge mazes. Ivy races her rival Pip from the checkered start to the trophy at the exit, through four worlds: Sky Gardens, Flooded Ruins, Crystal Caverns and Stormy Machinery. Every level is a fresh maze, and a Daily Maze gives everyone a new small maze each day.

Controls

  • Ivy runs by herself from GO. Swipe left or right to turn at the next opening; turn a moment early and she waits for the gap.
  • Swipe down to turn back. Running up to a laser, swipe down to slide under it.
  • Tap while Ivy runs to jump over sawhorses and flashing floor tiles.
  • At a warp door Ivy stops beside it: tap the door, or turn to face it and swipe up, to go through.
  • Two fingers and drag to look around.

How to play

  • The whole maze map stays on screen, with Ivy (green), Pip (red), the start flag and the finish trophy.
  • Glowing golden sneakers give a short speed boost. Pip can grab them too.
  • From level 2, flashing yellow floor tiles drop away for a few seconds. Wait at the edge or jump them: falling through ends the race.
  • Touch a laser and Ivy falls, gets up and races on. Pip is just as fast as Ivy, so every second counts.
  • Beat Pip to move to the next maze. Replay Run lets you race beside a ghost of your last attempt, and LEVELS lets you replay any level you've beaten.

Common questions

Where are the settings? Tap the pause button during a race, then SETTINGS. You can switch Music, Effects and Haptics on or off.

No sound? Check that Music and Effects are on and that your phone isn't on silent.

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your progress. There are no accounts and no cloud saves.

Does it need the internet? No. Sky Garden Maze Racer plays fully offline. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.
