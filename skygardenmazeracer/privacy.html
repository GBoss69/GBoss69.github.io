Sky Garden Maze Racer

Privacy Policy · Effective October 25, 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy
In short: Sky Garden Maze Racer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

Who we are

Sky Garden Maze Racer is made by Robert Rhatigan Jr, an independent developer. Contact: rhatpacklabs@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not connect to the internet, and it does not use any third-party services or SDKs that collect data. It does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (the level you've reached, best times, Daily Maze times, your last run for the ghost replay, and which tips and story you've seen) and your settings (music, effects and haptics) are saved only on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This information is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Sky Garden Maze Racer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Apple

When you download the app, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from Apple about you. If you have opted in to sharing crash reports and usage data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us, which we use only to fix bugs.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email rhatpacklabs@gmail.com.
